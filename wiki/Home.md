# Welcome to the Gradient and Reason Wiki

Gradient and Reason is an archival effort to bridge the gap between technical execution and deep understanding. This wiki houses the architectural blueprints, layout definitions, style guides, and essay creation workflows for the blog.

## Index of Documentation
* **[[Essay Guide]]**: Complete guidelines on how to draft, format, verify, and publish new essays.
* **[[Style Guide]]**: Details on the dark-academic aesthetics, color theme tokens, typography, and math rendering.
* **[[Codebase Structure]]**: Directory maps and explanation of layouts, dynamic scripts, and global state components.
