RUNNING EMPIRES OF THE MANIFOLD: VERIFYING HYPOTHESES
======================================================================
Generated 400 sensory experiences in 256-dimensional space.

--- EXPERIMENT 1: Testing Regularization Beta (beta-VAE) ---
  Beta= 0.0 | Recon=0.5661 | KL=12.0025 | Spread=2.7498
  Beta= 1.0 | Recon=0.6197 | KL=1.9214 | Spread=0.8862
  Beta=10.0 | Recon=8.6832 | KL=0.0000 | Spread=0.0000

>> As beta increases, KL drops and latent space collapses (spread -> 0).
   Perfect recall requires a disjoint space (beta=0); abstraction requires structural forgetting.

--- EXPERIMENT 2: Deleting Coordinates (Unlearning Force & SGD) ---
  Calculating instantaneous loss gradients on the base model weights...
  -> Isolated Unlearning Force (1 deletion):      0.005535
  -> Isolated Unlearning Force (cluster deletion): 0.960824
  -> Ratio: 173.59x greater gradient pressure

  Performing physical parameter updates using Vanilla SGD...
  -> L2 weight delta | 1 sample:  0.000051
  -> L2 weight delta | 1 cluster: 0.008190
  -> Physical impact ratio: 161.91x greater weight shift

>> With Adam's scale invariance bypassed, baseline gradients subtracted inside
   the step loop, and a surrogate loss used, cluster deletion produces a massive,
   proportional restructuring force versus a single experience coordinate.

--- EXPERIMENT 3: Decoder Drift (Temporal Shift of Meaning) ---
  Measuring Reconstruction Decay (MSE) of static coordinate through drifting decoders:
  -> Drift 0.01 | MSE degradation: 0.000083
  -> Drift 0.05 | MSE degradation: 0.001868
  -> Drift 0.10 | MSE degradation: 0.005641
  -> Drift 0.20 | MSE degradation: 0.028898

>> A 20x increase in drift (0.01->0.20) produces a 348x increase in
   reconstruction degradation. The relationship is superlinear — meaning decays
   faster than drift grows. Memory fidelity depends on the decoder speaking the
   same language as when the coordinate was encoded.
